Lucide line icon wrapper — use for every glyph in the UI; never emoji or hand-drawn SVG.

```jsx
<Icon name="music" />
<Icon name="volume-2" size={20} />
<Icon name="play" size={24} />            {/* transport glyphs auto-fill */}
<Icon name="info" title="Informações" />  {/* labelled */}
```

- Available: arrow-left, audio-lines, chart-no-axes-column, check, chevron-*, circle-alert, circle-check, circle-x, drum, expand, headphones, info, keyboard, list-music, loader-circle, mail, menu, minus, mouse-pointer-click, music, music-2, pause, play, plus, repeat, rotate-cw, send, settings, shrink, skip-back, skip-forward, square, timer, trash-2, triangle-alert, volume, volume-1, volume-2, volume-x, x.
- Sizes: 16 (inline/compact), 20 (default UI), 24 (transport/nav).
- Color inherits `currentColor`.
